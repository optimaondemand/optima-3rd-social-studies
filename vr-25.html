Scout for the Diorama · about 10 minutes

Scout the Bank

👀 What am I looking at? A bank is a place that keeps people's money safe. Money is something everyone agrees to take when they buy and sell. Good money lasts, is easy to carry, is easy to count and is trusted.
1Scout the place in VR→2Record your video→3Send it to Canvas

🖥️ Before your headset
Step 1 of 5 · Get ready
Money from the past: a silver dollar from 1795, money cowrie shells and a paper bill from 1861.Three kinds of money people have used.

In your video, you say these 2 sentences:

  1. 1This is a bank, and I see ___.
  2. 2People trust money because ___.

Practice now. Say both before the timer ends.

0:30

In VR, this page is on the Canvas tab too.

🥽 In VR
Step 2 of 5 · Go in
🔗 First time only: connect Canvas at xr.optimaed.com. See Having trouble in VR? below.

Open the On-Demand app. Click Explore. Type Bank in Filter environments. Click National Bank, then Begin Solo.

Step 3 of 5 · Scout the bank

Move with the thumbstick. Walk through the bank. Look at the counters, the doors and the walls. Look for places that keep money safe.

VR onlyExtra, if you have time
Slowly show the room while you record, so Opti sees how the bank keeps money safe.
Step 4 of 5 · Record your video and send it

Open your menu, click Library, then Camera. Move the tablet until the bank fits.

Click Record video. Say your 2 sentences. Click it again to stop.

Click the Canvas tab and pick 3.09.04 VR · Bank Vault Diorama. Click Choose from My Files, pick your video, click Use this file, then Submit to Canvas. Look for the green Submitted tag.

🔁 Want to fix it? Record a new video and send it again.
✅ After
Step 5 of 5 · Check and leave

Press the Meta button, then click Quit. Check that your video is in the Canvas assignment. Not there? Open Having trouble in VR?

⭐ Full points: One video, 30 seconds or less, that shows the bank while you say both sentences.
