Step 2 of 5 · Go in

Step 3 of 5 · Scout the court

Move with the thumbstick. Look at the columns, the steps and the big doors. If you can, go inside and look at where the judges sit.

VR onlyExtra, if you have time

Step 4 of 5 · Record your video and send it
