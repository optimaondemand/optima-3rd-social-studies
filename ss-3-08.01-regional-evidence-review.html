Five Region Evidence Cards

🗺️ Northeast

▼
Location:Upper-right corner, Atlantic coast
Landforms:Appalachian Mountains, Atlantic coastline, harbors and rivers
Resources:Fishing (lobster, cod), maple syrup, coal (historical), major seaports
⭐ Key Fact: Oldest cities in the country — Boston, New York City, Philadelphia

🌴 Southeast

▼
Location:Lower-right corner, Gulf + Atlantic coasts
Landforms:Coastal plains, southern Appalachians, Everglades, Mississippi River outlet
Resources:Citrus (Florida), peaches (Georgia), cotton, fishing
⭐ Key Fact: Warm winters; hot humid summers; hurricane season

🌾 Midwest

▼
Location:Center, interior plains
Landforms:Interior plains (Great Plains), Great Lakes, Mississippi River
Resources:Corn, soybeans, wheat ("Breadbasket of America"), automobile manufacturing
⭐ Key Fact: Harsh winters; dramatic seasons; tornado season

🏜️ Southwest

▼
Location:Lower-center and lower-left, desert/canyon country
Landforms:Desert landscapes (Sonoran, Chihuahuan), Rocky Mountain foothills, canyons, Rio Grande
Resources:Oil and natural gas, cattle, cotton (irrigated), tourism (Grand Canyon)
⭐ Key Fact: Hot and dry; very little rainfall; very sunny

🏔️ West

▼
Location:Left side, Pacific coast to Rockies
Landforms:Rocky Mountains, Cascade Range, Pacific coastline, diverse climates
Resources:Timber, salmon (Pacific Northwest), tech industry (California), tourism
⭐ Key Fact: Most diverse climate — rainforest in northwest, desert in southwest

The Big Picture
🏷️ Exhibit Label

The United States has five regions — Northeast, Southeast, Midwest, Southwest, and West — each with its own land, climate, resources, and character. A careful curator gathers evidence from every region before advising the council. The evidence shows that no region has everything it needs on its own.
