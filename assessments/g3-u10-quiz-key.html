1Multiple choice · 1 point · 3.10.01 Why Government Is Needed

What are the three big jobs a government does for a community?

  • A Selling toys, making games, and baking bread.
  • B Picking everyone's friends, food, and clothes.
  • C Keeping order, keeping people safe, and caring for the common good. ✔
  • D Collecting taxes and nothing else.

Why: A government keeps order (fair rules, like a property line), keeps people safe (like firefighters), and cares for the common good (things that help everyone, like a park).

2Multiple choice · 1 point · 3.10.02 U.S. Constitution and Power from the People

The U.S. Constitution starts with the words "We the People." Where does the government's power come from?

  • A The President.
  • B The people. ✔
  • C The army.
  • D The king of England.

Why: "We the People" means the power comes from the people. The people choose their leaders by voting, and the leaders must follow the Constitution too.

3Multiple choice · 1 point · 3.10.07 Civic Virtues

What is a constitution?

  • A A speech a leader gives on the first day of the job.
  • B A list of every law ever passed.
  • C A book of stories about a country's history.
  • D A written plan that sets up a government, gives it its jobs, and sets its limits. ✔

Why: A constitution is a written plan for a government. It sets up the government, gives it its jobs, and sets its limits. The U.S. and Florida each have one.

4Multiple choice · 1 point · 3.10.03 Local, State, and National Government

The trash on your street needs to be picked up. Which level of government does that job?

  • A Local government (your city or county). ✔
  • B State government (Florida).
  • C National government (the whole country).
  • D No level of government.

Why: Trash pickup happens right by your front door. Your city or county, the local government, sends the trash truck.

5Multiple choice · 1 point · 3.10.03 Local, State, and National Government

Which job belongs to the national government?

  • A Giving out Florida driver's licenses.
  • B Making the dollar bills used in every state. ✔
  • C Lending books at your town library.
  • D Picking up the trash on your street.

Why: Only the national government makes U.S. money, so one dollar works the same in all 50 states. Driver's licenses are a state job; the library and trash pickup are local.

6True or false · 1 point · 3.10.04 Florida Constitution and Florida Symbols

True or false: The Florida Constitution sets up Florida's state government, and the U.S. Constitution sets up the national government.

  • True ✔
  • False

Why: True. Florida has its own constitution for its state government, and the U.S. Constitution sets up the government for the whole country.

7Multiple choice · 1 point · 3.10.05 Voting and Elections

Why does a voting booth have a curtain?

  • A So the line moves faster.
  • B So nobody has to count the votes.
  • C So each voter can choose in private, and no one can watch or push them. ✔
  • D So only important people can vote.

Why: The curtain makes a secret ballot: each person votes in private. No one can watch, and no one can push them to pick something.

8Multiple choice · 1 point · 3.10.06 Patriotic Holidays and Observances

On July 4, Americans celebrate Independence Day. What happened on July 4, 1776?

  • A The Constitution was signed.
  • B Leaders approved the Declaration of Independence, saying the 13 colonies were free from Great Britain's king. ✔
  • C The first Veterans Day parade marched down Main Street.
  • D Florida became a state.

Why: On July 4, 1776, the Declaration of Independence was adopted. It said the 13 colonies were free from the king. The Constitution came later, in 1787.

9Pick all that are true · 1 point · 3.10.07 Civic Virtues

Which of these are civic virtues? Pick ALL that are true.

  • A Civility: treating people with respect, even when you disagree. ✔
  • B Being the loudest person in the room.
  • C Volunteerism: choosing to help your community without being told to. ✔
  • D Responsibility: doing what you said you would do. ✔

Why: Civility, volunteerism, and responsibility are civic virtues (so is cooperation). Being the loudest is not: civility means respect, not winning by shouting.

10Multiple choice · 1 point · 3.10.08 Symbols, Individuals, Documents and Events

In 1955, Rosa Parks calmly refused to give up her bus seat, even though she knew she could be arrested. What did she show?

  • A That only Presidents can be heroes.
  • B That she was tired of walking.
  • C That rules never matter.
  • D Heroism: great courage. ✔

Why: Rosa Parks showed heroism, great courage. Heroes are not only Presidents: any citizen can show heroism. Her choice helped change unfair bus laws.
