Scout the Steamship

👀 What am I looking at? A newcomer comes to live in a new country. Long ago, many came by steamship past the Statue of Liberty, a symbol of welcome.

🖥️ Before your headset
Step 1 of 5 · Get ready
What newcomers saw: the Statue of Liberty in New York Harbor. It stands for welcome.The Statue of Liberty, New York Harbor.

In your video, you say these 2 sentences:

  1. 1This is a ship that carried newcomers, and I see ___.
  2. 2The Statue of Liberty stands for ___.

Practice now. Say both before the timer ends.

0:30

In VR, this page is on the Canvas tab too.

🥽 In VR
Step 2 of 5 · Go in
🔗 First time only: connect Canvas at xr.optimaed.com. See Having trouble in VR? below.

Open the On-Demand app. Click Explore. Type Steam in Filter environments. Click Immigrant Steam Ship (Dusk), then Begin Solo.

Step 3 of 5 · Scout the ship

Move with the thumbstick. Walk along the deck. Look at where people would stand, sleep and wait. Look out at the water.

VR onlyExtra, if you have time
Look out over the rail while you record, like a newcomer watching for the new country.
Step 4 of 5 · Record your video and send it

Open your menu, click Library, then Camera. Move the tablet until the deck fits.

Click Record video. Say your 2 sentences. Click it again to stop.

Click the Canvas tab and pick 3.10.08 VR · Welcome Ship Diorama. Click Choose from My Files, pick your video, click Use this file, then Submit to Canvas. Look for the green Submitted tag.

🔁 Want to fix it? Record a new video and send it again.
✅ After
Step 5 of 5 · Check and leave

Press the Meta button, then click Quit. Check that your video is in the Canvas assignment. Not there? Open Having trouble in VR?

⭐ Full points: One video, 30 seconds or less, that shows the ship while you say both sentences.

5Explore tab
Click Explore. Type the map name in Filter environments.

Click the map, then click Begin Solo.
7Read the assignment
Press the top button on your right controller. Click the Canvas tab and pick our assignment. Tip: point at a mint side grip and squeeze to pull the tablet to your hand.
